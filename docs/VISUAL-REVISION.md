# Visual revision after review

The first implementation overemphasized typography and long explanatory sections. This revision gives the hardware and services more of the visible area.

Direction: a hardware showroom. Midnight #080f1a, blue #2867ed, silver #cad6e2, ice #eaf0f8 and white #ffffff. Existing Plus Jakarta Sans remains the display and reading face, with Sinhala and Tamil fonts retained.

Composition: short headline and actions above a wide hardware stage; a two-column gallery of service illustrations; compact pricing and process; a photographic business chapter. The founder information remains on About. This avoids making every section another row of copy and icons.

Service illustrations are original SVG components depicting a laptop and component, router with signal, printer and software window. They are decorative, with live translated headings and service links. Hover movement is disabled for reduced motion. The hardware hero retains the real optional Three.js viewer.

Business photography is the existing credited Pexels server-rack image, not claimed as DR TECH customer work. No generated people are used as evidence or staff portraits.

Tests cover existing routes, translations and request flow. Browser surfaces were unavailable during this revision; rendered desktop/mobile composition and WebGL still require visual review.
